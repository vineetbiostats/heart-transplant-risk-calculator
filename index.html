How the estimate is calculated

The ten category points are added. Predictions and confidence limits are read directly from the supplied score lookup table.

Risk(t) = 1 − S₀(t)exp(γ × ln(1.035802) × score)

γ = 1.0926029. Baseline survival: 0.96748726 (1 year), 0.92018142 (5 years), 0.84173748 (10 years), 0.72456804 (15 years).

Confidence intervals use 1,000 paired bootstrap recalibration estimates. They capture recalibration uncertainty conditional on the original scoring model being fixed, not uncertainty in original model development.
